About Pedex

Two businesses, one standard: reliable supply.

Pedex is a supplier of optical interconnect products and industrial flow-control equipment. Our optical division covers the full physical layer — transceivers, AOCs, fiber jumpers and fiber cables — serving telecom operators, datacenter integrators, system houses and regional distributors.

    Our Mission

    To be the supplier our customers call when a link needs to work on day one — and keep working for years after.

    Our Approach

    We don't try to do everything. We focus on optical components we understand well, and on the valve brands we have personally vetted. If a product isn't right for your application, we'll tell you.
